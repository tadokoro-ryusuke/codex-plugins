# Buyer Question Map

Map each buying decision to the search keywords and the AI prompts that express it, so search and AI-answer work share one plan. Start from the product, its customers, and recorded conversations; use search tools to check and extend the list, not to define the market.

## Decisions and questions

Use one row per decision a buyer has to make. Keep the prompt in the buyer's own words, including the situation and constraints that shape the answer, with names, company identifiers, and confidential details removed.

Column values:

- **Intent type** — problem, category, comparison or alternative, implementation, switching, brand.
- **Origin** — search console, support, sales or onboarding, community thread, review of a substitute, assumption. Mark assumptions so they can be replaced.
- **Existing page** — the URL meant to answer the question, or `missing`. A weak page and a missing page are different findings.
- **State in search** — not indexed, indexed but not ranking, ranking, with the evidence and date.
- **State in AI answers** — not measured, absent, mentioned, cited, inaccurately described, with the snapshot label.
- **Gap type** — content, technical, third-party source, none.
- **Priority** — rank by closeness to a purchase decision and by whether the product has a credible answer.
