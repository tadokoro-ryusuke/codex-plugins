Choose one decision per row:

- **Preserve** — it works; leave it alone.
- **Improve for search** — the answer is good but the page is not found.
- **Improve as an answer source** — the page is found but does not answer the question directly or verifiably.
- **Overhaul** — both are weak.
- **Create** — no focused page can do the job.
- **Collect more evidence** — the data cannot yet support a decision.

Do not create a page for every keyword or prompt variation, and do not rewrite a page that already works.

## Answer-source review

Use these checks to fill the "usefulness" column. They make a page easier for a reader and for an engine to quote accurately. They are not known to produce citations by themselves; see the evidence base.

- [ ] The opening says what the product is, who it is for, and when to use it.
- [ ] The page answers the priority question directly, then the adjacent ones a buyer asks next: alternatives, price, limits, compatibility.
- [ ] Facts are specific, dated, and checkable. Prices and limits are stated outright.
- [ ] Each key statement stands on its own, without depending on context elsewhere on the page.
- [ ] Claims that need support cite a primary source.
- [ ] Facts match the facts of record and what other sites say about the product.
- [ ] The visible update date reflects a real change in content.
- [ ] Structured data matches the visible text.
- [ ] Limitations are stated; unsupported superlatives are removed.
