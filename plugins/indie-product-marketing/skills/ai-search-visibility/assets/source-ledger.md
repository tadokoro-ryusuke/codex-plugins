# Third-Party Source Ledger

Track the specific pages, threads, reviews, and listings that shape answers to priority buyer questions, what each says about the product, and the one action chosen for it. Fill it from the domains the engines actually cited in a snapshot, not from a list of platforms assumed to matter.

Column values:

- **Type** — own page, own profile, directory or review listing, comparison or list article, community thread, press, documentation, video.
- **State** — absent, inaccurate, outdated, incomplete, fair.
- **Route** — Own, Claim, Earn, Contest, Leave. Choose one.
- **Action** — the single next step, written so that someone else could carry it out.
- **Disclosure or label** — how the relationship or payment will be shown where the reader sees it, or `none needed`.
- **Rule checked** — the platform or community rule and the date it was read.
- **Result** — what changed on the source, with the date. A request that was declined is a result.

## Actions that need the user's explicit instruction

Drafts can be prepared at any time. Do not perform these without a direct request for that action:

- [ ] Posting, replying, or messaging in a community or on social media
- [ ] Sending a review request or any other email, by hand or through a platform's invitation tool
- [ ] Contacting an author, publisher, or site owner
- [ ] Claiming a profile or submitting a listing
- [ ] Agreeing to a paid or exchanged placement
- [ ] Proposing an edit on a wiki talk page

Never carry out these, whether or not they are requested: posting without disclosing the relationship, posting from accounts that are not the user's own, coordinated voting, and automated or bulk posting, replying, following, or messaging in communities.
