## Timeline

### T-14 to T-7

- [ ] Observe seed users and resolve first-value blockers.
- [ ] Freeze positioning, offer, and source naming.
- [ ] Verify core events, cohorts, payment, and support.
- [ ] Obtain community and partner permission.
- [ ] Capture current conversion and quality baselines.

### T-6 to T-1

- [ ] Prepare demo, product page, FAQ, email, and channel-native assets.
- [ ] Verify disclosures, privacy, tracking consent, and store/platform rules.
- [ ] Rehearse launch, incident, rollback, moderation, and refund handling.
- [ ] Confirm who monitors support and metrics.

### Launch day

- [ ] Notify the highest-intent owned audience first.
- [ ] Publish the native product demonstration in the primary channel.
- [ ] Respond to qualified questions and product friction.
- [ ] Record defects, complaints, source quality, and unusual traffic.
- [ ] Avoid changing multiple major variables during the measurement window.

### T+1 to T+7

- [ ] Fix reliability and activation failures first.
- [ ] Compare source-level activation and early retention.
- [ ] Interview converters and qualified non-converters.
- [ ] Update messaging only when evidence supports the change.

### T+8 to T+30

- [ ] Review natural-interval retention and payment cohorts.
- [ ] Run bounded onboarding, offer, product-page, and channel experiments.
- [ ] Reconcile proceeds, fees, refunds, variable cost, support, and founder time.
- [ ] Decide whether to scale, revise, concentrate, or stop.
